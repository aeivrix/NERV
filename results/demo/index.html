Mind Drift Replay

A 10-second EEG meter, swept across a whole meditation session every 2 seconds, then checked against what the meditator actually said each time a voice asked. Every session is scored by a model that never saw that person.

probe answer: mind wandering probe answer: meditating in between shaded trace: meter, 30 s average · gaps: Q&A or artifacts

How to read it

Above the line, the last 10 s look more like this person's mind-wandering moments; below, more like their settled ones. The scale is relative to the session, not absolute.

It is a weak signal. Across everyone, a wandering probe outranks a meditating one from the same person about 60% of the time (50% is a coin toss). Read the trend, not single wiggles.

Drowsiness and time on task explain part of it, and its accuracy differs a lot between people.

Any 64-channel BioSemi recording. It cleans the signal, sweeps the meter and writes a CSV and a plot next to it.
