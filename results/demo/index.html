What the study found

AUC within each person, tested on people the model never saw. 0.50 means it can't tell the two states apart. Across all replays the meter's sign matches the answer 53% of the time; ranked within a person, 57%.

0.49

The reaction gives nothing away

The brain's first second of hearing the question doesn't predict the answer. Same in a second lab (0.49), and with Riemannian and EEGNet models.

0.60

The 10 s before is a weak clue

Significant here (p = 0.021), partly time on task, and not replicated on ds008836 (0.54). It's the meter in the player above.

0.92

"Expert" decoders read sex

Experts are mostly men, novices mostly women. EEG tells sex apart; expertise (0.69) isn't significant beyond it.

530

Zero ratings hiding in plain sight

events.tsv silently drops every 0 answer (530 in Q3 alone). True labels were recovered from the behavioural logs.

How the meter works

Nothing tuned on results: the analysis was frozen in git before the model saw any real labels.

Clean

0.1–40 Hz filter, bad channels found and rebuilt, eye and muscle components removed with ICA.

Read 10 seconds

Theta, alpha and beta power on all 64 channels, scaled against the rest of that session.

Score without you

A shrinkage LDA trained on everyone except the person being replayed.

Sweep

A new reading every 2 s. Q&A moments and jaw clenches are left blank instead of guessed.

Run it on your own recording

Any 64-channel BioSemi file. You get a CSV of readings and a plot.

uv run python -m probe_eeg.meter --bdf my_session.bdf
